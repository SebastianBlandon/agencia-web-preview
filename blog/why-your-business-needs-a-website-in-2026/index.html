Why your local business still needs a website in 2026

2026-10-17

Most customers search before they call. If the first thing they find is an outdated Facebook page, you lose them to the competitor with a clear site, visible phone number and reviews.

What a good local site actually needs

  • One clear headline that says what you do and where.
  • Your phone number and a WhatsApp or booking button above the fold.
  • Three to six services with plain-language descriptions.
  • Real reviews with first names.
  • Address, hours and a map link.

What it does not need

A blog you will never update, a chatbot, or animations that slow the page down. Speed and clarity convert.
