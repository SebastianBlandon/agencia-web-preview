Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Páginas web, Cali, Colombia. Contact: johan@wepago.com.

What we collect and why

  • When you write to us (email or WhatsApp): your name, business details and message, to reply and prepare your preview or quote. Legal basis: steps prior to a contract at your request.
  • If you become a client: billing and project details, to deliver the service and meet tax obligations. Legal basis: contract and legal obligation.

If we contacted you first

We may email businesses whose contact details are publicly listed by the business itself (its website, Google Business Profile or public directories), only at generic business addresses, to offer a website preview. Legal basis: legitimate interest in business-to-business communication. We do not buy lists or collect data from personal social media. Every message identifies us, and you can opt out at any time by replying "STOP"; we then delete your details except a suppression record so we never write again.

How long we keep data

Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 12 months. Client records: as long as required by tax law.

Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the service (email, hosting, payment processor (Wepago)). Some are located outside your country, including the United States; we rely on their standard contractual safeguards. We never sell personal data.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, or object to its use, by emailing johan@wepago.com. EU residents may also complain to their data protection authority. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics. If that changes, we will use a cookieless, privacy-friendly tool and update this page.
